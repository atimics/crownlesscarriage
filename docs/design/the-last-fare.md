# The Last Fare

Status: proposed later expansion.

This page is a short guide to the expansion. The full design is kept in
[issue #271](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/issues/271) and its
[second part](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/issues/271#issuecomment-5547278436).
Those records hold the detailed rules, tuning proposals, research, and open work.

## The journey

The company accepts a funeral commission to carry a bell keeper home to
Bellfield. Near the cemetery, the horses stop at an infested road. The company
can leave the team at a road house, carry supplies, and continue on foot.
Clearing the crossing lets the coffin and other travellers reach their destination.

Coffins, graves, zombies, the Last Choir, and Veyr's lich power connect this
journey to royal authority, bandit tolls, goblin pacts, and family claims.

## Planned work

- Road walking, with supplies, hazards, return points, and a common clock.
- Coffin handling, transport, burial, and records of who holds each body and item.
- Zombie encounters and horse responses to infested roads.
- Road authority and faction agreements with named parties, terms, and means of enforcement.
- Veyr's power, supported by bound souls, a vessel, relics, bells, keepers, and supplies.
- An open lich campaign with rest, an oath, and destruction as possible resolutions.

## Build order

The 5 September 2026 plan places this expansion after the
[route work in #315](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/issues/315) and
[the mill adventure in #268](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/issues/268).
The first expedition needs road walking, a common clock, body and item custody,
and faction road authority.

Start with one complete Bellfield funeral journey. Potter's Field and the abbey
follow it. Resolve the death-rule overlap in
[#286](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/issues/286) in light of
[#334](https://github.com/atimics/crownlesscarriage/pull/334)'s twenty-year
transition after a full party loss. Issue #271 tracks the remaining expansion work.
