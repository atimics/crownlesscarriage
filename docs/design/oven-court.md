# Silverwick's Oven Court

The court makes an existing production chain readable. It does not create a
quest, fuel recipe, delivery reward, automatic recovery, or a second inventory.

## Ordinary controls

Approach **Oven Court tally**, on the Company store frontage, and Inspect by
pointer, object card, or E/F. Inspecting acquires a dated Company Book note.
Tally shows the local stores and current next-act bottleneck; What helps?
distinguishes missing Wheat, absent capacity, rebuilding, reserved grain,
full Bread storage, and a supplied but not-yet-executed batch.

Company store starts an ordinary walk to the existing door, not a teleport.
Use the existing counter and Sell tab for actual quantities and payment, or
Bakery for the existing support/repair rules. Wheat and Bread retain their
other uses. No shipment or purchase is reserved by inspecting the tally.

An eligible, living, present baker or the existing bakery-support receiver
can answer **What do the ovens need?** through the character reply path.
They consult the same public tally; arbitrary residents are not granted its
knowledge. This authored reply remains usable without a language model.

B opens the Court page from inspection. Latest/Earlier retain two acquired
notes. Reading a saved note, selling supplies, or someone changing a distant
store never rewrites its date or text. Re-inspect locally to learn a change.
A delivery is not proof of baking, exclusive player credit, or full recovery.

## Boundaries

- CcSimPlanBakery and scheduled baking use the same plan and recipe. Preview
  copies stock; it does not spend inputs, time, random state or emit work.
- The first mining settlement (Silverwick in the authored start) is the pilot.
  The tally intentionally publishes that local communal store/readiness, not
  remote incoming manifests, private accounts or exhaustive cause history.
- Acquired notes are self-addressed CC_EVENT_LORE_RECORDED records in the
  existing event tape. Their actor distinguishes a copied public tally from
  a named speaker. The newest two are pinned during ordinary compaction.
  No new save schema or independent knowledge ledger is introduced.
- OBSERVE_OVEN_COURT validates current town, travel/expedition state and source
  eligibility. Local spatial reach uses the existing interaction/approach
  system. It does not introduce a separate server-side spatial authority.
- The new shallow facade uses the same primary-hall wall convention and anchor
  as the interaction. Visible loaves are a capped stock cue, not individual
  takeable objects. A boarded oven follows missing bakery service. No flame,
  smoke or work animation fabricates a production receipt.
- The four-town art PR remains independent; navigation and service anchors are
  unchanged. This is not a rebuild of the town geometry or a new interior.

## Dialogue provenance

The Python typed-fact adapter now requires independent acquisition metadata
(`source`, defaulting conservatively to `told`). Confidence cannot become
witnessing. The renderer qualifies an uncertain eyewitness without changing
how the account was acquired. Legacy categorical tokens are retained; this is
not a new checkpoint, training run, or native typed-fact policy promotion.
Historical demonstration reports are not rewritten as new measurements.

## Verification

`oven_court_economy_and_memory` checks read-only plans, input versus capacity,
existing grain reserves, finite sales, unchanged dated evidence, source/presence
checks, replay, event compaction, and shared/direct command agreement.
`oven_court_input_flow` starts at a staged Silverwick position and exercises
ordinary keyboard inspect, Book, walking to door and counter, existing sale
confirmation and a grounded reply. It is not a human playtest. Existing trade
terms tests retain stale-price/quantity coverage.

`tools/capture_oven_court.py` renders six explicitly staged economic views and
two large-text variants. It rejects a reused output folder, validates PNG
size, records hashes/commands/revision, and preserves a partial manifest on
failure. The drawings are the game renderer, not generated concept art.
