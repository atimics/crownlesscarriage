# Town-to-trade touch proof

The browser journey starts a fresh campaign in Thornford, enters Granary Hall, buys two Bread through the touch controls, saves with F5, reloads, and verifies the saved purse and cargo.

The quote before confirmation was 8 crowns. The receipt charged 8 crowns. The purse changed from 42 to 34, and cargo changed from empty to two Bread. After reload, the purse remained 34 and the carriage still held two Bread.
