# Solution for Issue #271

## 🛠️ Proposed Solution

### Analysis
The issue is a design proposal for a future expansion, not a bug fix. The requested action is to keep the design as a reference and ensure it is preserved for later implementation.

### Fix
Create a dedicated design document in the repository that captures the full scope, mechanics, and narrative hooks. This file will serve as the authoritative reference for the expansion.

### Implementation
```markdown
# The Last Fare – Design Document

## Overview
The Last Fare is a Crownless expansion focused on coffin handling, graveyards, zombies, a lich, and foot travel along roads. It expands the road book to support travel on foot alongside carriage travel.

## Core Themes
- **Road Safety** – Players must navigate an infested road, dealing with zombies and hostile factions.
- **Coffin Mechanics** – Handling, transporting, and burying bodies.
- **Faction Pacts** – Interactions with royal authority, bandit rule, goblin pacts, and family claims.
- **Lich Power** – A central antagonist that ties the journey together.

## Key Features
1. **Road Walking** – Mechanics for walking along roads, including checks for hazards and encounters.
2. **Coffin Work** – Rules for building, transporting, and burying coffins.
3. **Zombie Encounters** – Encounters that consume players and create a sense of danger.
4. **Faction Interaction** – Structured interactions with factions that can aid or hinder the party.
5. **Lich Campaign** – A potential open-ended campaign centered on the lich’s power.

## Narrative Hooks
- **Bellfield Funeral** – A complete funeral journey before the Potter’s Field and the abbey.
- **Death-Rule Overlap** – Resolve the overlap in #286 after #334’s 20‑year transition.
- **Royal Action** – Funding and support from royal authority.

## Design References
- #255 – Existing world and road reference.
- #268 – Opening road adventure.
- #132 – Road signs and return points.
- #216 – Shared interaction.
- #205 – Road blocks.
- #208 – Scribe appraisal.

## Next Steps
- Draft detailed mechanics for each feature.
- Integrate with existing road book.
- Prepare a playtest module.
```
